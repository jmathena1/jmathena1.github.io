Who owns the vacant homes in Baltimore City?

Marimo notebook calculating what percentage of houses with vacant building notices are owned by entities or people with local mailing addresses. I pulled the data in June 2025.


The City That Works

In 2023, I conducted an exploratory analysis of Baltimore City employee salary data. I wanted to calculate basic aggregate measures like median, minimum, and maximum salary, tenure, and average raise year over year broken down by city agency. I also wanted to demonstrate how to conduct an end-to-end data analysis project using publicly available data. I published my results in a Jupyter notebook and converted the notebook into an html document. The resulting report is published on this site. Click the project view button below to check it out!





Can you afford to work for Baltimore City?

In a follow up to “The City That Works”, I built a data app using the Observable Framework allowing users to see which city agencies pay a living wage depending on the household make up (number of adults working and number of children). I used the MIT Living Wage Calculator to determine living wages for Baltimore city. The report uses city salary data and living wage data for 2024.
