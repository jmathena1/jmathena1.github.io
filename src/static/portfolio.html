Who owns the vacant homes in Baltimore City?

Marimo notebook calculating what percentage of houses with vacant building notices are owned by entities or people with local mailing addresses. I pulled the data in June 2025.


Can you afford to work for Baltimore City?

Observable Framework data app allowing users to see which city agencies pay a living wage depending on the household make up. MIT Living Wage Calculator data from 2024 used to determine living wages for Baltimore city.


The City That Works

Exploratory analysis of Baltimore city employee salary data using Jupyter and Google BigQuery. Salary data covers the early 2000s through about 2022.
